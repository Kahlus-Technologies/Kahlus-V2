## Sections 2.2 to 2.4 of e1-design.md, copied unchanged

### 2.2 Arms, sizes, data

- **Arms.** `signal`: the current loss (masked patch + 4 s future, mean squared error on the signal). `latent`: the same encoder, masks and heads, but the targets are the hidden states of a slow copy of the encoder (code spec C2).
- **Sizes.** `10m`, `50m`, `200m` from `kahlus.model.SIZES`. No `1b`: the corpus has about 310M tokens.
- **Compute match.** One step of `latent` costs about 4/3 of a `signal` step [estimate]. So `signal` runs 19,200 steps and `latent` runs 14,400 steps, both at 512 windows per step on 1 GPU. 19,200 steps is 4 passes over all training nights. Measured GPU-seconds are reported for every run.
- **Training pool P.** Shard nights of `/shared/aayu/fill-a/shards/shhs1-full` that are not `is_heldout` and not dev IDs 200001 to 200025.
- **Data cells, all at the same steps.** With `u = sha256("e1:shhs:<id>")` scaled to [0, 1): `r1` = P, `r4` = `u < 1/4`, `r16` = `u < 1/16`.
- **Quality axis.** For each night, from the EDF, per 1 s patch of C4 and ECG. A patch is bad if one of these holds:
  - flat: its standard deviation is under 1% of the night's median patch standard deviation of that channel;
  - railed: a sample lies within 0.5% of the header's physical minimum or maximum;
  - burst: C4 peak-to-peak is over 10 times the night's median.

  `q` = 1 minus the bad fraction. Split P into quartiles of the arousal index (arousals per hour of sleep, from the XML). In each quartile, `clean4` = the top 25% by `q` and `dirty4` = the bottom 25%, ties by `u`. So the two sets have the same arousal-index mix.
- **Grid.** 5 data cells x 3 sizes x 2 arms x seeds 0, 1, 2 = 90 runs.
- **Held-out subjects.** `is_heldout` (1 in 10, about 579) are never in pretraining or probe fitting. MESA is never read by a training run.

### 2.3 Readout

- Anchors: every 30 s epoch boundary. Window: 64 s of both channels that ends at least 2 s before the anchor (the pilot A rule).
- `detect` (primary): 1 if a scored arousal overlaps the last 10 s of the window, 0 if no arousal lies within 30 s before or after it, else left out. Sleep epochs only.
- `stage` (fallback primary and secondary): 5 classes, as in pilot B.
- Probe: logistic regression on the standardised embedding of the last second, `C` from {0.01, 0.1, 1} by log loss on the tune subjects. Fit = the 300 fit subjects with the smallest `u`. Tune = the 100 `is_tuning` subjects with the smallest `u`. Test = all held-out subjects.
- Metric: test log loss in bits, per subject. Rows for band power, random weights (3 seeds per size) and the base rate.

### 2.4 Gates and kill rules

- **G0, range gate (before any new pretraining).** On the existing full-data 10M checkpoint, the `detect` probe must beat random weights with an interval above 0. If not, `stage` becomes the primary. If `stage` also fails, E1 stops.
- **GQ, quality gate.** The median bad fraction of `dirty4` must be at least 3 times that of `clean4` and at least 0.01 higher. If not, the two quality cells are dropped and the report says SHHS-1 has no quality contrast by this metric.
- **K1, collapse.** A `latent` run is marked collapsed if its `target_std` ends under 0.05, or if its tune-subject probe is not better than random weights of the same size. A collapsed run stays in the table as the result. If all 10M `latent` runs of step 8 collapse, the `latent` arm is reported as failed and the grid runs `signal` only.
- **K2, flat after 50M.** If no arm at 50M beats its 10M result on the primary at `r1` (interval above 0), 200M runs at `r1` only (6 runs, not 30).
- **K3, budget.** Hard cap 900 GPU-hours. If step 8 timings project more, cut in this order: 200M seeds 3 to 2, then `dirty4` at 200M, then `r16` at 200M.
- **K4, baseline.** If no encoder beats band power on the primary at the end, the headline is that negative result. No rescue runs.
